# Changelog

## 0.18.1 – November 19, 2026

- Proledo no longer stops when your Mac is in Low Power Mode. It keeps playing and uses the lighter copy of a video if there is one. If you want it to stop on battery, there's still a switch for that in Settings.

## 0.18 – November 19, 2026

- New wallpaper style: Nebula. Colorful clouds of teal, blue, violet and pink slowly drift and swirl behind the stars. The colors keep moving across the whole screen, so it stays as gentle on PROLED and OLED displays as silk.

## 0.17.1 – November 18, 2026

- Turning off motion for a display now keeps the picture on screen and just stops the video, instead of going black. "For all" and generating for all displays include that display again, so everything still looks the same.

## 0.17 – November 18, 2026

- You can now turn videos off for a single display, handy if only one of your screens is OLED. That display stays out of "for all" actions and generating for all displays, and your old setting comes back when you turn it on again.

## 0.16 – November 17, 2026

- Silk looks smoother: the dark shades fade into each other without visible steps.
- Silk is easier on OLED screens too. The spots that used to light up the most now get a bit less, while the overall look stays the same.

## 0.15 – November 17, 2026

- The menu is tidier now: displays, library and settings each have their own tab.
- The library is a compact list grouped by style, so it's easier to find things.
- All settings are in one place instead of hiding in a little menu.

## 0.14 – November 17, 2026

- Proledo can now cover a screen completely when you've been away for a few minutes, windows included. Move the mouse and it's gone. Turn it on per display.
- The generator can make a video for every display at once, each in the right size, and sets them up right away.
- Rotation can stick to one style, so all your screens look the same.

## 0.13 – November 17, 2026

- New wallpaper style: silk. Dark satin layers slowly fold and drift across the screen, with a soft sheen and bright edges. Because the whole thing keeps moving, it's one of the gentlest styles for OLED screens.

## 0.12 – November 17, 2026

- The northern lights look a lot more real now: fine rays of different lengths, a bright lower edge, a hint of red up high and stars shining through.
- Fireflies flash up quickly and fade slowly like real ones, and they gather above a dark meadow.
- Both are even easier on OLED screens than before.

## 0.11 – November 17, 2026

- Two new wallpaper styles: northern lights swaying over a dark mountain ridge, and fireflies drifting around and glowing up one by one.
- The style is now picked from a menu, so there's room for more.

## 0.10 – November 17, 2026

- Rotating videos got smarter: landscape displays only rotate through landscape videos and portrait displays through portrait ones, and two displays never show the same video at the same time.
- Right-click a video in the library to keep it out of the rotation.

## 0.9 – November 17, 2026

- New wallpaper style: a starry sky. Stars drift slowly in three layers, a faint Milky Way sits in the background (you can turn it off) and every now and then a shooting star goes by.
- The wallpaper preview now has the shape of the display you're making it for.

## 0.8.1 – November 16, 2026

- Proledo now lives at github.com/Pixzl/Proledo. Links and update checks point there.

## 0.8 – November 16, 2026

- A calmer, more Mac-like menu: plain sections instead of boxes, standard switches and buttons, fewer hints.
- Playback has a simple on/off switch at the top.
- Pick a display's video right next to its name. Hiding the menu bar and Dock are regular switches now.
- Fixed a display tile spilling over the others when a portrait video played on a landscape screen.

## 0.7.2 – November 16, 2026

- Adding your own file or making a wallpaper now has two proper tiles instead of small text links.
- The README finally has pictures.

## 0.7.1 – November 16, 2026

- Nicer buttons: the link field has the paste button built in and a round arrow to continue, and all other buttons share the same rounded look.

## 0.7 – November 16, 2026

- Displays now show up as a little map, arranged just like your real setup. Click one to set it up.
- Let a display rotate through all your videos, every 10 minutes up to every 2 hours. More variety, even less burn-in.
- The library is now a grid with bigger previews.
- Settings moved behind a gear icon to keep things tidy.
- Proledo lets you know when a new version is out.
- Proledo is now MIT licensed.

## 0.6 – November 16, 2026

- Deleting a video now moves it to the Trash under its own name, so nothing is gone for good by accident.
- You can add several video files at once.
- A short welcome guide shows up while your library is still empty.
- Picking a video for a display shows little previews and a checkmark next to the current one.
- Creating a wallpaper keeps going in the background if you close it, and the progress shows at the top of the menu.

## 0.5 – November 16, 2026

- Proledo can now make wallpapers on its own: flowing contour lines on black, made to fit the exact shape of the display you pick, portrait screens included. Choose how dense, how fast and how bold, hit Create, done.
- Generated wallpapers loop seamlessly and have no watermark, so there's nothing static left to burn in.
- The app now speaks English, German, French and Spanish, depending on your Mac's language.

## 0.4 – November 16, 2026

- You can rename videos now. Double-click a name in the library, use the pencil icon or right-click and pick Rename.

## 0.3 – November 16, 2026

- New power saving mode: Proledo makes a lighter copy of each video (30 fps, up to 1440p) and plays that instead. Your Mac does noticeably less work, and the original stays untouched.
- With power saving on, new downloads default to 1440p.
- The menu bar icon is now a little screen instead of something that looked like a record button.

## 0.2 – November 16, 2026

- The app is now called Proledo and has a new, simpler icon.
- Long videos can be downloaded as a clip, so the file stays small.
- Before downloading you see a preview, the length and a rough size.
- The menu bar and Dock can be hidden per monitor.
- Videos fade in and blend into each other.
- Right-click the icon for a small quick menu.
- Proledo can start automatically when you log in.

## 0.1 – November 16, 2026

- First version: a YouTube video as a moving wallpaper, set per monitor.
